Corporate Profile

About Gogna Enterprises

Reliable supply chains, authenticated metallurgical quality, and long-standing partnerships with builders, fabricators, and infrastructure developers.

Our Heritage & Vision

Two Decades of Engineering Discipline in Steel

Gogna Enterprises was established with a singular objective: to provide regional contractors and manufacturing industries with dependable access to primary-grade structural steel and reinforcement rebar.

Over the last 25 years, our enterprise has expanded from a local merchant stockist into a full-scale distribution hub holding thousands of metric tonnes of ready-to-ship inventory across five covered warehouse bays.

Whether supplying 500 tonnes of Fe 550D rebar for a highway flyover, high-tolerance ISMC channels for an industrial PEB warehouse, or precision steel plates for heavy machine tools, our team ensures punctual delivery, transparent commercial invoicing, and complete compliance documentation.

Guiding Principles

The Values That Shape Our Business

Every order we fulfill reflects our commitment to structural safety, commercial transparency, and operational excellence.

Uncompromising Quality

We never supply sub-standard or re-rolled scraps. Every batch of TMT bars, angles, channels, and plates complies with BIS standards (IS 1786, IS 2062).

Fair Wholesale Pricing

Transparent, competitive daily market billing with no hidden loading charges or arbitrary delivery penalties. Transparent project budgets start here.

Punctual Logistics Fleet

Our company-operated fleet of flatbeds and articulated trailers ensures prompt, safe transit right to the builder's jobsite or fabrication shop floor.

End-to-End Solutions

Complete Value-Added Steel Services

Beyond standard steel trading, Gogna Enterprises offers specialized processing services designed to reduce fabrication cycle times, minimize jobsite scrap, and optimize material utilization.

✓

Custom Cutting & Profiling

In-house CNC flame cutting and high-speed cold sawing for angles, beams, and heavy plates up to 100mm.

✓

Bar Bending Schedule (BBS) Support

Custom cutting and pre-bending of TMT reinforcement rebar according to structural engineering schedules.

✓

Consolidated Project Packages

Single-source supply of structural beams, purlin channels, roofing sheets, fasteners, and rebar for PEB structures.

✓

Certified Computerized Weighment

State-certified electronic weighbridge providing tamper-proof gross, tare, and net tonnage slips.
